# STUDENT-HUB-PORTAL
An all-in-one student hub for GPA &amp; CGPA tracking, expense management, financial planning, event planning, and academic predictions.

## ✨ Features

### 📊 GPA Calculator

Calculate your GPA based on your course scores, grades, and credit units.

### 🎯 CGPA Prediction

Predict your potential CGPA based on your current academic performance and planned results.

### 💰 Expense Tracker

Record expenses and compare the amount spent with your available cash, making it easier to keep track of your spending.

### 🔮 Expense Prediction

Estimate future expenses to help with planning and managing your available funds.

### 📅 Event Planner

Write down and organize planned events such as parties, academic activities, scholarship opportunities, and other important events.

### ✅ Task Completion

Mark planned events as completed once they're done, making it easier to keep track of what's been accomplished.

## 🎯 Purpose

Student life involves juggling academics, finances, deadlines, events, and everyday responsibilities. The Student Hub Portal brings some of these tasks together into one simple platform.

The goal is to give students a convenient way to keep track of both their academic progress and personal planning without having to rely on multiple separate tools.
